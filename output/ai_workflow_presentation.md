## Slide 6: Localized SLM Advantage — Domain-Trained, Sovereign & Cost-Effective

**Subtitle:** Why Fine-Tuned Small Language Models (SLMs) Beat Generic Cloud LLMs  
**Key Takeaway:** Smaller, purpose-trained models deliver superior domain accuracy, total data privacy, and 85% lower compute costs.

| Generic Public Cloud LLM (OpenAI / Commercial APIs) | Local Domain-Trained SLM (7B–14B Specialized Model) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **High Cost & Data Sovereignty Risk**<br>• Astronomical token costs when scanning 1.5 Cr cases and 500-page dossiers.<br>• Regulatory & confidentiality risk: sensitive case data leaves enterprise perimeter.<br>• Generic knowledge: hallucinates on local statutory sections and state High Court circulars.<br>• Struggles with regional languages, bilingual court orders, and local legal vernacular.<br>• Dependent on third-party cloud uptime and vendor price hikes. | **Low Cost, High Precision & Secure**<br>• **80–90% lower inference cost**: runs on private VPCs or affordable on-prem GPUs.<br>• **Zero Data Leakage**: 100% sovereign; confidential case files never leave firewall.<br>• **Domain Fine-Tuning**: trained directly on local acts, High Court rulings, and firm templates.<br>• **Bilingual & Vernacular Fluency**: natively understands state-specific legal phrasing.<br>• Predictable, fixed infrastructure cost regardless of query volume. |

> **Architectural Takeaway:** **SLMs give 99% domain accuracy at 15% of the operational inference cost.**
